Pick a character in the list to plan its raids. The list shows each character's gold for the week and the roster total.
Configure your run by selecting the right mode: Solo, Normal, Hard or Nightmare. Not available modes (based on the raid and your ilvl) are greyed out.
For each raid you're taking gold in game, tick the Taking Gold checkbox. A character earns gold from at most 3 raids a week, and only the 6 weekly gold characters set on the Roster page earn gold.
If you are buying the chest, tick the Taking Chest checkbox.

You can further customize each gate of a raid by expanding the gates using the + icon. Mixed means that you mixed and matched modes.

Interested in how much gold you can still earn this week? Set the Full Planning slider to Remaining for the week and find out. The remaining raid entries are based on your weekly completion in the Checklist.
