In short

Lost Ark Checklist is a free fan-made tool. It stores only what it needs to run your checklist and keep it safe. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored and why

  • Account email, only if you register, so you can sign in on other devices. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication, which keeps only a secure hash of it.
  • Username you choose when you register.
  • Characters: names, classes, item levels and the options you set on the Roster page.
  • Checklist state: your tasks, ticks and rest bonus.
  • Settings: your choices on the Settings and Gold Planner pages.

This data is used only to run your checklist, keep it in sync between your devices, and protect the site against abuse. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Who processes it

The site runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting). Google processes the data on this site's behalf, as a processor. Firebase Authentication also processes your IP address and browser details to keep sign-in secure; Google keeps IP addresses for a few weeks.

Firebase App Check uses Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise, also as a processor, to check that requests come from this site and not from bots. This site is protected by reCAPTCHA and the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in the United States.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Guest accounts are not deleted automatically either. After you delete your data, Google removes it from its backups within 180 days.

Your choices

  • Get a copy: on Settings, Bring data over, Download backup saves your characters, checklist state and settings as a file. It does not include your username or account email, which you entered yourself.
  • Correct it: change anything on the Roster, Tasks Manager and Settings pages.
  • Erase it: on Settings, Delete my account and data removes everything stored for your account, then the account itself. For a guest, the data is removed and the browser starts a new, empty guest account.

You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Storage in your browser

The site keeps your sign-in session and an App Check token in your browser (IndexedDB), a few display preferences in local storage (for example whether the sidebar is collapsed), data saved by older versions of the site until it is moved to your account, and whether you closed the guest banner in session storage. reCAPTCHA stores a _grecaptcha entry for security. All of this is strictly necessary for the site to work and stay secure, so there is no cookie banner.
